## Promises the copy cannot keep

No user-facing string prints a price or a dollar amount. That check passed on README, `src/`, `server.json`, `glama.json`, and the live HTML for `/`, `/connect`, `/terms`, `/privacy`, `/access`, `/support`, and `/data`.

These sentences are not kept by the behavior above:

- README: "When the allowance is used up, Fillip says so and does not send more until it resets." Creating a reminder does say so. A reminder that is already due and then hits the cap does not, and two workers can both send when one email remains.
- README: the trial allowance is "during the 14-day trial." The counter resets at UTC month start, and the stand-in is 30 per month.
- README: the idempotency key means "a retry does not send a second copy." The key is stable, and a provider that honors it stores one inbox copy. A crash after accept still submits a second HTTP call, and two workers reclaiming a failed row both decide to send.

"A heavier month cannot cost more than that plan pays for" was not contradicted by a charge path in this pass. Terms do not guarantee delivery.

## What could not be tested without live hosting

- Real OAuth inside ChatGPT and Claude. This repo does not implement dynamic client registration, PKCE, or those clients' redirect URIs. `supabase/README-oauth.md` only says to add `APP_BASE_URL` and `APP_BASE_URL/oauth/consent`. JWT signature checks are delegated to Supabase `GET /auth/v1/user` and were mocked. Audience and expiry were checked in `validateMcpClaims` against that mock.
- Real Resend. Idempotency was tested with an in-process sender that dedupes on `Idempotency-Key` after recording the call. The 15-minute back-off was the timestamp Fillip stores and later jobs consult. Resend's own 429 body, retry, and rate limit were not called. The empty-count failure was reproduced with the HTTP 416 PostgREST returns for `Range: 0-0` on zero rows.
- Real Stripe. Signature checks used a local HMAC and `whsec_qa`. Delivery retries, Dashboard replays, and a shared live account with Continuity or Desk were not available. The product-id filter was tested with fixture events only.
- Vercel cron and Supabase `pg_cron` racing each other in production. README says either is enough. `vercel.json` schedules `GET /internal/deliver` every minute. Both firing is the concurrent-worker case already reproduced in process.
